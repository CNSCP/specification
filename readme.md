# CNS/CP Specification

CNS/CP — the Connectivity Naming System using Connection Profiles — defines what must be true for two independently owned systems to find one another, establish that what one offers matches what the other needs, and come to be connected on terms both have accepted, under common governance. It specifies what is observable between the parties at each step, and nothing about how any implementation achieves it.

## The current text

**[cns-cp.md](cns-cp.md)** — the **2026 Revision**, Sections 1 to 10 with two informative appendices. Editors: Toby Considine and Anto Budiardjo.

Its status is stated on its own title page and is repeated here so that nobody reads more into this repository than it holds:

- It is a **clean reading copy, assembled 14 September 2026** from the canon working drafts. The canon drafts remain the source of truth; this document is regenerated from them and is never edited directly.
- It is a **working draft, published for information**. It is not a contribution to any standards body and no text in it is final.
- Its body has been reviewed by the CNS/CP pre-working group, most sections more than once, and on 14 September 2026 the group raised no comment on the substantive points drafted since the previous full review. The open points that remain are listed on the title page; none is structural.

Comments are welcome. Please cite the section number.

## Conformance

Section 9 defines conformance for four targets — a Governor, a Registry, a Connection Profile and a Node — and is the clause that the CNS/CP licensing terms refer to when they speak of a conforming implementation.
